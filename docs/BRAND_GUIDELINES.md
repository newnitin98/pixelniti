# Brand Guidelines

## Brand Personality
Premium, Strategic, Intelligent, Clean, Futuristic (but not childish), Trustworthy, Practical, Business-focused. Indian-origin but globally usable.

## Visual Identity Concept
- **Logo Direction:** A clean "P" mark, a pixel grid, a strategy node, or a geometric monogram.
- **Visual Metaphor:** Pixels forming a strategic path, digital blocks becoming business outcomes, or a clean network graph.
- **Avoid:** Cartoonish AI robots, overly flashy neon designs.

## Color Palette
- **Background:** Deep navy / near black.
- **Primary Text:** White and soft grey.
- **Accents:** Electric blue, violet, or emerald.
- **Subtle Highlights:** Smooth gradients.
- **Cultural Hint:** Optional warm accent such as saffron/gold used very sparingly to hint at Indian origin.
- **Avoid:** Overuse of neon colors, generic plain reds/blues/greens.

## Typography
- **Primary Font:** Modern sans-serif (e.g., Inter, Outfit, or Plus Jakarta Sans) for clean legibility and a tech-forward feel.
- **Style:** Strong, highly readable headings with subtle tracking; balanced, clean body text.

## UI Elements
- **Button Styles:** Clean shapes, subtle glowing borders or gradients on hover, solid and trustworthy appearance.
- **Card Styles:** Glassmorphism used tastefully (not overdone), subtle dark borders, soft glow effects on hover.
- **Icon Style:** Lucide React icons, stroke-based, minimal, abstract.
- **Animation Style:** Framer Motion micro-interactions, smooth fade-ins, gentle translations.
- **Images/Illustrations:** Abstract pixel/strategy/AI visuals, digital product mockups. Avoid generic stock photos or fake office teams.

## Footer Credit Style for Client Websites
The standard footer credit text used on client websites will be:
`Website crafted by PixelNiti`
(Linked to https://pixelniti.com).
This should be elegant, legible, and non-intrusive.
